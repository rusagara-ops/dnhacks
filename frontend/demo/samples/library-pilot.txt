Library Evening Access Pilot

The fictional Brookfield City Library will run a three-month evening-access pilot from October through December. On weekdays, the library will close at 9 p.m. instead of 6 p.m. Saturday hours will remain unchanged, and the library will remain closed on Sundays.

The city council approved $18,000 for additional staffing. This funding covers only the pilot; it does not authorize a permanent extension of opening hours. Library director Maya Chen will oversee the program.

A free digital skills workshop will take place every Tuesday from 6:30 p.m. to 8 p.m. Participants will learn how to complete online job applications, create a basic resume, and use public-service websites. Twelve computers will be available. Residents must reserve a place by phone or at the front desk. The workshops are open to adults with any level of computer experience.

Staff will record evening attendance, workshop participation, operating costs, and visitor feedback. The target is an average of 40 visitors per evening and at least eight participants per workshop. These are targets, not results: the pilot has not started yet.

In January, Maya will present an evaluation to the council. The council will then decide whether to continue, modify, or end the extended hours. No decision about permanent funding has been made.
